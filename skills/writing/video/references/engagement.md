# Engagement design

Design authored and edited video for maximum viewer engagement: earn attention early, sustain it with useful progress, and deliver the promised payoff. Record the intended viewer, platform, payoff and primary engagement goal in the brief. Infer missing context from the source and user directions, state assumptions and proceed. Default to sustained attention and completion; optimize sharing, saves or a requested action only when the brief supports it. Narrow edits still stay narrow.

- **Hook:** make the first complete thought establish a concrete benefit, problem, surprising result or relevant question. For short-form, aim to earn attention in the first 1–3 seconds; for longer lessons, establish the payoff within roughly 5–10 seconds. These are planning targets, not unsafe cut boundaries. Remove expendable greetings and setup. In engagement-focused edits, one self-contained source passage may move to a cold open without repeating it later or implying an unsupported result. Record the new adjacency and check its meaning; preserve prerequisite order.
- **Progress:** map hook → essential context → explanation/demo → payoff. Each section advances understanding, supplies evidence or resolves a question. Preserve prerequisites before dependent steps. Deliver useful information throughout; a curiosity-building question must get an answer.
- **Pacing:** remove repetition and idle time before speeding up speech. Keep the shortest complete take and concrete example, with time to understand technical material and read results. Vary section length with the content rather than imposing a fixed cut interval. Useful progress per minute matters more than minimum runtime.
- **Attention cues:** use demonstrations, reveals and changes of subject to renew attention. Captions, B-roll, zooms, music and slides must fit the selected workflow's authorized scope. Clean edits keep them opt-in; requested launch, meme and Shorts workflows retain their own treatments. Add elements for comprehension or emphasis, not constant stimulation.
- **Payoff:** resolve the opening promise and finish promptly after the useful conclusion. Preserve a complete ending, truth and qualifications. A source-backed call to action serves the brief rather than extending an already-finished video.

Before delivery, account for the opening, each section's contribution, the payoff and retained pacing risks in the current brief. Revise weak points within the authorized scope using existing evidence and required Jev gates. This is an engagement design check, not a perceptual review or proof of audience response. Claim improved retention or engagement only with actual viewer analytics.
